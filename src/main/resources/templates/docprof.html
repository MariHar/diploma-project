About Dr. Emily Chen

Dr. Emily Chen is a board-certified cardiologist with over 15 years of experience in treating complex cardiovascular conditions. She specializes in interventional cardiology, heart failure management, and preventive cardiology.

Dr. Chen graduated magna cum laude from Harvard Medical School and completed her residency in Internal Medicine at Johns Hopkins Hospital, followed by a fellowship in Cardiology at the Mayo Clinic. She is passionate about providing patient-centered care and staying at the forefront of cardiovascular medicine.

Her research has been published in numerous peer-reviewed journals, and she is a frequent speaker at national and international cardiology conferences. Dr. Chen is committed to educating her patients about heart health and empowering them to make informed decisions about their care.
